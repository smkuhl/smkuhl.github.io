I'm a postbaccalaureate researcher at the Allen Institute for Immunology with a strong foundation in machine learning and multiomics data integration. I am interested in developing interpretable machine learning models that bridge large-scale omics data with immune phenotypes to better understand the mechanisms underlying human health and disease.

I currently develop and apply computational modeling and statistical analysis to investigate gene regulatory mechanisms in immune cells. I've presented findings at an international scientific conference attended by 500+ researchers and co-authored 3 manuscripts.

Aside from research, I love to read, sew and play the French horn!
