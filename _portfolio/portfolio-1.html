--- title: "Generative AI for Music" excerpt: "RNN model that composes music based on Bach chorale training data
" collection: portfolio --- ![Johann Sebastian Bach](/images/Bach-GettyImages-919644932.jpg) Photo: Fine Art Images/Heritage Images/Getty Images This project implements the Recurrent Neural Network (RNN) architecture to generate original music based on Bach chorale training data. Bach chorales are four part works that are relatively short and simple as they generally consist of one or two musical phrases. Example of model output: RNN model improves over previous as it is able to learn long-term dependencies. However, the model struggles to complete phrases.
